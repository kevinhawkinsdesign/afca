About AFCA

An alliance built by and for the people electrifying Africa's roads.

Who We Are

AFCA was formed to bring coherence to Africa's rapidly growing electric vehicle charging landscape. We bring together charge point operators, automakers, energy utilities, standards bodies, and government agencies to solve the shared challenges of interoperability, financing, and grid readiness.

What We Do

Set Shared Standards

We coordinate on open protocols so charging hardware and software work together across networks and borders.

Advocate for Policy

We engage regulators and ministries to align incentives, tariffs, and permitting with a growing EV market.

Build Capacity

We run workshops and knowledge exchanges so operators and technicians can learn from one another.

Track Progress

We publish research and data on charging infrastructure growth across African markets.

Our Values

Openness

Interoperable systems over walled gardens.

Equity

Reliable charging access for every market, not just the largest cities.

Collaboration

Shared problems deserve shared solutions.
