{/* BIKENEWE: gusimbuza n'ibiri ku Kenya by'ukuri, birimo CPO, ubushobozi bw'ikibaho, n'imiterere y'amabwiriza. */}

Iki ni ikiganza cy'agateganyo cyerekana urugero rw'incamake y'igihugu. Ibiri by'ukuri bizerekana
CPO zikora, ubushobozi bw'ikibaho, imikorere y'icyuzuzo mu buzima nyabwo, n'ishyirwa mu bikorwa
ry'amabwiriza, buri mubare ukaba ufite isoko ryawo bwite hakurikijwe uburyo bwa AfCA bwo gukoresha
amasoko.
