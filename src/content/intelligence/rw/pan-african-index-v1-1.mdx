Iki ni ikiganza cy'agateganyo cyerekana verisiyo iriho ubu y'urugero rw'iraporo ya Index.
Ibishushanyo, amakarita, n'imbonerahamwe zuzuye zizasimbura iki gice igihe amakuru y'ukuri
azaboneka; iyi shusho itanga urwego rw'kwerekana gusa.

## Ibyahindutse uhereye kuri v1.0

- Ubugari bwagutse buva ku bihugu bitatu bugera ku bihugu bitanu.
- Kuboneka ubu bipimwa nk'ukuboneka mu gukoresha (niba igikorwa cyagerageje cyarangiye), aho
  kuba uko igicuruzwa kigerwaho gusa — reba <a href="/rw/standards/cpms">amabwiriza ya CPMS</a>
  kugira ngo umenye impamvu iyi tandukaniro ifite agaciro.
