Powering Africa's Electric Mobility Future

The African Charging Alliance (AFCA) brings together charge point operators, vehicle manufacturers, utilities, and policymakers to build an open, interoperable, and reliable EV charging network across the continent.

Join the Alliance

Our Mission

Accelerating equitable access to electric mobility infrastructure across Africa through shared standards, knowledge, and advocacy.

Interoperability

We champion open charging standards so drivers can charge anywhere, on any network, with any vehicle.

Policy & Advocacy

We work with governments and regulators to create the conditions for sustainable charging infrastructure investment.

Knowledge Sharing

We connect operators and innovators across markets to share what works, from Cairo to Cape Town.
