One plug shouldn't decide where you can drive.

An EV bought in Lagos can't always charge on a network built for Nairobi. AFCA exists to fix that: shared technical standards, one policy voice, and a table where operators, automakers, utilities, and regulators talk to each other before the continent's charging map hardens into forty incompatible ones.

Join the Alliance

What we actually do

Not another conference circuit. Three concrete jobs, worked on a schedule, with member organizations doing the work alongside us.

Interoperability

We write the shared protocols so a plug from one network authenticates on another without a support call. Working groups, not white papers — the target is a spec operators can actually ship.

Policy & Advocacy

We sit with regulators while the rules are still being drafted, not after. Tariff structures, import duty on charging hardware, grid-connection permitting — the unglamorous line items that decide whether a business case pencils out.

Knowledge Sharing

An operator who solved battery-swap logistics in Kigali has already answered a question someone in Accra is about to ask. We move that knowledge before it gets re-learned the expensive way.
