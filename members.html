Membership tiers

Operator Member

For charge point operators and network providers running chargers today. Seat at the technical working groups, direct input on the shared standards you'll be asked to support.

Industry Partner

For manufacturers, utilities, and suppliers building the hardware and grid capacity underneath the network. Early access to research, market data, and advocacy briefings before they go public.

Institutional Member

For government bodies and NGOs shaping the rules the rest of us build inside of. Direct engagement on policy and infrastructure planning, not a once-a-year courtesy briefing.
